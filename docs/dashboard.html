Two are answered, and the biggest one dissolved

Frappe HR turns out not to need a private bench — it ships with shared hosting. So the migration can start now, and hosting becomes a later decision about enforcement rather than a gate on everything.

Answered

Hi-Tech Pretreads is Manna Treads

Same company, not created separately. 112 rows load into Manna Treads, joining the 4 already there — which makes it the second largest employer in the group rather than the smallest.

Two left to create: Hi-Tech Rubber Industries, Manna Group H-QTRS.

Blocking

Manna Tyre UAE is on an Indian clock

Measured across all 114 punches: every Indian rep agrees with the server to the second, and all three UAE reps are out by exactly 90 minutes. The app stamps the handset's timezone, not the site's.

Separate site, or fix the app and treat UAE as display-only?

Later

Enforcement, not hosting

A private bench is needed only for our own rules app. Coordinates and the server's own creation stamp are recorded either way, so misuse can be detected today — just not refused.

First ask Frappe whether Server Scripts can be switched back on. That answer is free.

Decide soon

Selfie on every punch

Factor HR photographs each mobile punch — the sample export carried 35 images for 34 punches. At 160 people that is roughly 5 MB a day, held until someone sets a retention rule.

Keep it, drop it, or keep it only for field staff?

Fingerprint machines

The integration works, and the hard part is already done

The employee master carries a Machine Code — the join between a fingerprint template and a person. It is the one field that could have sunk this, and it is populated and unique.

Ready

139 of 160 have a machine code

139 enrolled 21 missing

Missing: Hi-Tech Pretreads 9, Manna Rubber Products 7, Manna Tyre Retreads 3, Manna Group H-QTRS 1, Manna Treads 1. Some will be office staff who punch on a phone; the rest need enrolling.

Verified

No duplicate codes

Every machine code is unique among active staff. This is the failure that cannot be recovered from afterwards — two people sharing a code means their punches were never distinguishable.

Nothing to fix here.

Two sources, one stream — which is exactly how ERPNext wants it

Sequence

What happens next, in the order it has to happen

The first three are done. Each remaining step still depends on the one above it — loading people before the holiday calendar exists gives every one of them a working day that nobody checked.

  1. Upgrade the plan and install Frappe HRDone

    hrms 16.16.0 is live. Employee Checkin, Shift Type, Attendance, Leave and Payroll all present and empty.

  2. Create the two missing companiesDone

    Hi-Tech Rubber Industries (HRI) and Manna Group Headquarters (MGHQ). Six companies now.

  3. Turn on geolocation, add departments and employment typeDone

    GPS capture was off by default. Now on, plus 21 departments and the Permanent employment type.

  4. Move the site to a private benchManna

    One button on the site page. Costs nothing extra, and Server Scripts switch on by themselves. Do it out of hours, and before 160 people start punching.

  5. Send the holiday calendarManna

    Nothing can compute a working day without it, and a missing festival marks the whole factory absent on a day they were right to be at home.

  6. Ten minutes on the designation listManna

    33 names, straight from Factor HR's capitals. They become permanent labels in ERPNext.

  7. Import 160 employeesUs

    File is built and mapped clean, nothing rejected. Two passes, because a manager must exist before anyone reports to them.

  8. Walk through the 23 shiftsBoth

    Starting with the 22- and 24-hour patterns. Most likely step to take longer than expected.

  9. Bridge the fingerprint machinesBoth

    Needs the device list: make, model, IP per site. Runs on a machine inside your network.

  10. Run both systems for one monthBoth

    Then reconcile person by person before Factor HR is switched off.
